Past Simple Tense
যেসব কাজ অতীতে শেষ হয়ে গেছে — যেমন "yesterday" (গতকাল), "last week" (গত সপ্তাহে), "two days ago" (দুদিন আগে) — তা বোঝাতে Past Simple Tense ব্যবহার হয়। এই lesson-এ regular এবং irregular verb-এর past form শিখবেন।
🧩 Structure
Affirmative
Subject + Verb(2nd form / -ed) + ...
I watched a movie last night.
Negative
Subject + did not (didn't) + Verb(base form) + ...
She didn't go to school yesterday.
Question
Did + Subject + Verb(base form) + ...?
Did you finish your homework?
📘 Regular Verbs (+ -ed)

সাধারণত verb-এর শেষে -ed যোগ করলে past form হয়।

PresentPast (+ed)Example
PlayPlayedI played football yesterday.
WatchWatchedShe watched TV last night.
WalkWalkedWe walked to school.
CookCookedMom cooked rice.
StudyStudiedHe studied for the exam.
StopStoppedThe car stopped suddenly.
💡
Spelling rules: "y" থাকলে "i" হয়ে "ed" (study→studied); একটি vowel + consonant হলে consonant দ্বিগুণ হয়ে "ed" (stop→stopped); "e" দিয়ে শেষ হলে শুধু "d" (live→lived)।
⚡ Common Irregular Verbs

অনেক verb-এর past form একদমই আলাদা — এগুলো মুখস্থ করতে হয়।

PresentPastExample
GoWentI went to the market.
EatAteWe ate dinner together.
SeeSawShe saw a movie.
DoDidHe did his homework.
HaveHadThey had a great time.
ComeCameMy friend came yesterday.
TakeTookI took a photo.
MakeMadeShe made a cake.
WriteWroteHe wrote a letter.
BuyBoughtWe bought new shoes.
📌 Was / Were (Past of "to be")
SubjectPast Be VerbExample
I / He / She / ItwasI was at home yesterday.
You / We / TheywereThey were happy.
I was tired last night.
আমি গত রাতে ক্লান্ত ছিলাম।
Were you at the party?
তুমি কি পার্টিতে ছিলে?
It wasn't raining yesterday.
গতকাল বৃষ্টি হচ্ছিল না।
🕓 Time Signal Words

📝 Practice Quiz
1. I ___ (go) to school yesterday.
goed
went
go
✅ ঠিক! "Go" এর past form "went" (irregular)।
2. She ___ (not/eat) breakfast this morning.
don't eat
didn't eat
not ate
✅ সঠিক! Negative-এ "didn't + base verb" হয়।
3. ___ you watch the movie last night?
Did
Do
Was
✅ ঠিক! Past simple প্রশ্নে "Did" ব্যবহার হয়।
4. "Study" এর past form কী?
studyed
studied
studing
✅ সঠিক! "y" থাকলে "i" হয়ে "ed" — study → studied।
5. They ___ very happy at the wedding.
was
were
is
✅ ঠিক! "They" এর past be verb হলো "were"।
