📝 Mastery Quiz — 15 Questions (A1 to C2)
1. [A1] ইংরেজি Alphabet-এ কতটি Vowel আছে?
4
5 (A, E, I, O, U)
6
✅ ঠিক! A, E, I, O, U — ৫টি vowel।
2. [A2] "She ___ very happy at the party." (Past be verb)
was
were
is
✅ সঠিক! She/He/I + was।
3. [A2] "I will call you tomorrow." — এটি future-এর কোন ব্যবহার?
তাৎক্ষণিক সিদ্ধান্ত বা প্রতিশ্রুতি
আগে থেকে পরিকল্পিত
অতীতের ঘটনা
✅ ঠিক! "will" = immediate decision or promise।
4. [B1] "I ___ (live) in Dhaka since 2015." সঠিক form কোনটি?
lived
have lived
was living
✅ সঠিক! "since" + Present Perfect — have lived।
5. [B1] "The letter was written by her." — এটি কোন voice?
Active Voice
Passive Voice
Reported Speech
✅ ঠিক! was + written by = Passive Voice।
6. [B1] "You should see a doctor." — "should" কী বোঝাচ্ছে?
পরামর্শ
বাধ্যতামূলক নিয়ম
সম্ভাবনা
✅ ঠিক! "should" = advice/recommendation।
7. [B2] "If I had studied harder, I ___ passed." (Third Conditional)
would pass
would have passed
will pass
✅ সঠিক! Third Conditional: would have + V3।
8. [B2] "I am tired." → She said that she ___ tired. (Reported Speech)
was
is
am
✅ ঠিক! Reported Speech tense backshift: am → was।
9. [B2] "Sales rose sharply." — Graph-এ এটি কী দেখায়?
দ্রুত বৃদ্ধি ↑
ধীরে হ্রাস ↓
স্থির →
✅ ঠিক! rose = increased, sharply = rapidly।
10. [C1] Academic writing-এ "I think" এর পরিবর্তে কোনটি উপযুক্ত?
I strongly feel
It can be argued that
Obviously,
✅ সঠিক! "It can be argued that" — impersonal academic phrasing।
11. [C1] "You can't trust him — he failed school." — কোন logical fallacy?
Ad Hominem
Slippery Slope
False Dichotomy
✅ ঠিক! ব্যক্তিকে আক্রমণ করা = Ad Hominem।
12. [C2] "Her eyes were stars." — এটি কোন figurative device?
Simile
Metaphor
Personification
✅ সঠিক! "like" ছাড়া সরাসরি তুলনা = Metaphor।
13. [C2] "Ask not what your country can do for you..." — এটি কোন rhetorical device?
Anaphora
Antithesis
Tricolon
✅ ঠিক! বিপরীত ধারণা পাশাপাশি = Antithesis।
14. [C2] Research paper-এ "data were collected" — "were" কেন?
Passive voice তাই
"data" plural noun তাই plural verb
Past tense তাই
✅ ঠিক! "data" = plural → "were collected"। Passive-ও ঠিক, কিন্তু কারণটা plural agreement।
15. [C2] Aristotle-এর rhetoric-এ "Logos" কী?
আবেগে আবেদন
বক্তার বিশ্বাসযোগ্যতা
যুক্তি ও তথ্যের মাধ্যমে persuasion
✅ ঠিক! Logos = logical argument, evidence, data।
