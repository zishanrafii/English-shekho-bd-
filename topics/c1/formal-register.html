Register ও Tone নিয়ন্ত্রণ
Register মানে ভাষার আনুষ্ঠানিকতার মাত্রা — কার সাথে কথা বলছেন এবং কোন context-এ তার উপর নির্ভর করে কোন ধরনের ভাষা ব্যবহার করবেন। C1 স্তরে আপনি অনায়াসে formal ও informal register-এর মধ্যে switch করতে পারবেন।
📊 Register-এর স্তর
Registerকোথায় ব্যবহারউদাহরণ
Frozen / Legalচুক্তি, আইনি দলিল"Whereas the party of the first part..."
FormalAcademic, business, official letters"I am writing to request..."
Neutralসংবাদ, সাধারণ লেখা"The government announced a new policy."
Informalবন্ধু, পরিবার"Hey, did you hear about the new policy?"
Slang/Colloquialঘনিষ্ঠ বন্ধু"Yeah, totally insane right?"
🧑‍⚖️ Formal Register-এর বৈশিষ্ট্য
FeatureInformal ❌Formal ✅
ContractionsI'm, don't, won'tI am, do not, will not
Phrasal Verbsfind out, put off, go onascertain, postpone, continue
Sentence LengthShort, simpleLonger, complex
First person"I want to...""It is requested that..." / "One may..."
Exclamations"That's amazing!""This is a noteworthy development."
Discourse markers"Also, And, So""Furthermore, Moreover, Therefore"
🔄 একই ভাব — ভিন্ন Register
Informal: "Sorry I can't make it to the meeting."
Formal: "I regret to inform you that I will be unable to attend the meeting."
Informal: "Thanks for your help!"
Formal: "I am most grateful for your assistance in this matter."
Informal: "Can you send me the report?"
Formal: "I would be grateful if you could forward the report at your earliest convenience."
Informal: "The results are really good."
Formal: "The results demonstrate a significant level of improvement."
💼 Professional Writing Tips
Open with the purpose immediately.
প্রথম বাক্যেই কেন লিখছেন তা বলুন — পরিচয় বা small talk এড়ান।
Use full forms, not contractions.
"I am writing" — "I'm writing" নয়।
Avoid emotional language.
"I was shocked" নয় — "This raises significant concerns."
Be concise — cut unnecessary words.
"Due to the fact that" → "because"; "at this point in time" → "now"।
💡
Key Rule: Always match your register to your audience and context — over-formality in casual settings sounds unnatural, and under-formality in professional settings seems disrespectful.
📝 Practice Quiz
1. Formal writing-এ "find out" এর পরিবর্তে কোনটি ব্যবহার হবে?
look up
ascertain
check out
✅ ঠিক! "ascertain" = formal version of "find out"।
2. "Sorry I can't come." — এর formal version কোনটি?
I won't be there, sorry!
I regret to inform you that I will be unable to attend.
Can't make it, my bad.
✅ সঠিক! Formal register-এ full forms ও polite, impersonal language ব্যবহার হয়।
3. "Due to the fact that" এর concise formal alternative কোনটি?
because
since the reason is
owing to the fact
✅ ঠিক! "Due to the fact that" = unnecessarily wordy → simply "because"।
4. Formal writing-এ contractions এড়ানো হয় কেন?
কারণ এগুলো English নয়
কারণ এগুলো informal ও casual মনে হয়
কারণ এগুলো grammatically ভুল
✅ ঠিক! Contractions (I'm, don't) informal — formal writing-এ full forms ব্যবহার হয়।
