Professional Negotiation Language
Negotiation — চাকরি, ব্যবসা, বেতন আলোচনা বা দ্বন্দ্ব নিরসনে — একটি অত্যন্ত মূল্যবান দক্ষতা। সঠিক ভাষা ব্যবহার করলে আপনার position স্পষ্টভাবে তুলে ধরা যায়, সম্পর্ক নষ্ট না করেও।
🔄 Negotiation-এর পাঁচটি ধাপ

1. Opening

সম্পর্ক স্থাপন ও উদ্দেশ্য বলা।

"Thank you for meeting with us today. Our goal is to find a mutually beneficial solution."

2. Exploring

উভয় পক্ষের চাহিদা বোঝা।

"Could you clarify what your main priorities are?"

3. Proposing

প্রস্তাব রাখা।

"What if we were to offer...?" / "We could consider..."

4. Bargaining

শর্ত নিয়ে আলোচনা।

"If you can agree to X, we'd be willing to Y."

5. Closing

সমঝোতায় আসা।

"So, to summarise what we've agreed upon..."
💬 Key Negotiation Phrases
উদ্দেশ্যPhrase
প্রস্তাব নরম করা"What if we were to...?" / "Would you consider...?"
শর্ত রাখা"If you can agree to... then we would be prepared to..."
সময় চাওয়া"I'd like to take some time to consider this." / "Could we revisit this point?"
না বলা নম্রভাবে"I'm afraid that's not something we're able to agree to at this stage."
মধ্যপন্থা খোঁজা"Perhaps we could find a middle ground." / "Is there room for compromise?"
চুক্তি নিশ্চিত করা"So we're agreed on..." / "Let me confirm what we've settled on."
🎯 Softening & Diplomatic Language

Negotiation-এ সরাসরি "No" বা strong rejection এড়িয়ে diplomatic ভাষা ব্যবহার সম্পর্ক ভালো রাখে।

Direct (এড়ানো উচিত)Diplomatic (ব্যবহার করুন)
"That's impossible.""That might be challenging for us, however..."
"No, we can't do that.""I'm afraid we're not in a position to agree to that."
"That's too expensive.""We were hoping for a more competitive figure."
"You must lower the price.""Is there any flexibility on the pricing?"
💬 Sample Negotiation: Salary Discussion

Candidate: Thank you for the offer. I'm genuinely excited about this role. However, based on my experience and the market rate, I was hoping for something closer to 60,000 BDT per month.

Manager: I understand your position. At this stage, the budget for this role is set at 52,000. Is there any flexibility on your end?

Candidate: I appreciate that. If we could agree on 56,000, along with a performance review in six months, I'd be very happy to accept.

Manager: Let me see what I can do. I think 55,000 with a review at the six-month mark might be possible. Would that work for you?

Candidate: That sounds like a reasonable middle ground. I'd be pleased to accept that.

📝 Practice Quiz
1. "That's impossible." এর diplomatic alternative কোনটি?
No, absolutely not.
That might be challenging for us, however...
We refuse to consider this.
✅ ঠিক! Diplomatic language দিয়ে "no" বলা সম্পর্ক ভালো রাখে।
2. Negotiation-এ শর্ত রাখার সঠিক structure কোনটি?
If you can agree to X, we'd be willing to Y.
You must do X or we leave.
We want X and that's final.
✅ সঠিক! "If X, then Y" — conditional structure দিয়ে শর্ত নরমভাবে রাখা হয়।
3. "Is there any flexibility on the pricing?" — এটি কী বোঝাচ্ছে?
দাম বাড়ানোর অনুরোধ
দাম কমানোর সম্ভাবনা আছে কিনা জিজ্ঞেস করা
দাম গ্রহণ করা
✅ ঠিক! "flexibility" জিজ্ঞেস করা = নম্রভাবে কম দাম চাওয়া।
4. Negotiation closing-এ কোন phrase ব্যবহার হয়?
What are your priorities?
So, to summarise what we've agreed upon...
What if we were to offer...?
✅ ঠিক! "To summarise what we've agreed..." closing-এ চুক্তি নিশ্চিত করে।
