Reading comprehension মানে একটি লেখা পড়ে এর মূল ভাব, বিস্তারিত তথ্য এবং লেখকের উদ্দেশ্য বুঝতে পারা। এই lesson-এ একটি ছোট article পড়ে কিছু প্রশ্নের উত্তর দিন।
🧠 Reading Strategies

1. Skimming

পুরো লেখাটি দ্রুত চোখ বুলিয়ে মূল বিষয় বোঝা।

2. Scanning

নির্দিষ্ট তথ্য (যেমন তারিখ, নাম) খুঁজে বের করা।

3. Context Clues

অজানা শব্দের অর্থ আশেপাশের বাক্য থেকে অনুমান করা।

4. Main Idea vs Details

মূল বিষয় (main idea) এবং সহায়ক তথ্যের (details) মধ্যে পার্থক্য বোঝা।

📄 Read the Article: "The Benefits of Reading"

Reading is one of the most useful habits a person can develop. It is not just a way to pass time — it has many real benefits for the brain and for daily life.

First of all, reading improves vocabulary. When people read books, newspapers, or articles, they meet new words every day. Over time, this makes their writing and speaking much stronger.

Secondly, reading reduces stress. Many studies have shown that reading for just twenty minutes can lower stress levels more than listening to music or taking a walk. This is because reading requires full attention, so the mind forgets its worries for a while.

Finally, reading widens a person's knowledge of the world. Through books, readers can learn about different cultures, histories, and ideas without leaving their homes. For students, this knowledge often helps them perform better in exams and discussions.

In conclusion, although reading might seem like a small activity, its benefits are huge. Anyone who wants to improve their language, reduce stress, and gain knowledge should try to read a little every day.
