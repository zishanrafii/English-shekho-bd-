Direct → Indirect Speech
কেউ কী বলেছে তা অন্যকে জানাতে গেলে Reported Speech (Indirect Speech) ব্যবহার হয়। এতে tense, pronoun এবং time/place expression পরিবর্তন হয়। বাংলায় আমরা বলি "সে বলল যে..." — ঠিক একইভাবে English-এও কাজ করে।
🔁 Direct vs Reported Speech
Direct Speech (সরাসরি)
She said, "I am happy."
Reported Speech (পরোক্ষ)
She said that she was happy.
💡
লক্ষ্য করুন: Quotation marks উঠে গেছে। "I" → "she" হয়েছে। "am" → "was" হয়েছে। "that" যোগ হয়েছে।
⏮️ Tense Backshift (Tense এক ধাপ পিছিয়ে যায়)
Direct SpeechReported Speech
Present Simple → "I work here."Past Simple → she said she worked there.
Present Continuous → "I am eating."Past Continuous → he said he was eating.
Past Simple → "I visited Dhaka."Past Perfect → she said she had visited Dhaka.
will → "I will call you."would → he said he would call.
can → "I can help."could → she said she could help.

❓ Reported Questions

প্রশ্নকে Reported Speech-এ রূপান্তর করলে question order উলটে statement order হয়।

WH-Question
Direct: She asked, "Where do you live?"
Reported: She asked where I lived. (question mark নেই)
Yes/No Question
Direct: He asked, "Are you ready?"
Reported: He asked if I was ready. ("if" যোগ হয়)
📢 Reported Commands & Requests
Command → told + to + V1
Direct: "Sit down!" → Reported: She told him to sit down.
Request → asked + to + V1
Direct: "Please help me." → Reported: He asked her to help him.
